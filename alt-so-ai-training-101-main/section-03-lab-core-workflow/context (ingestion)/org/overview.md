# Mary's Place Overview

## Organization
- Nonprofit operating family shelters and housing-stability programs in Seattle and King County.
- Works to ensure that no child sleeps outside.

## Mission
- Ensure that no child sleeps outside by centering equity and opportunity for women and families.
- Help families regain stable housing through emergency shelter, mobile outreach, diversion, and housing supports.

## Vision
- A community where all families have safety, stability, and housing.

## Values
- **Love:** Welcome, respect, accept, and care for every guest.
- **Collaboration:** Partner across the region to expand quality services and elevate family voices.
- **Equity:** Foster inclusive environments and equitable access and opportunity for all.
- **Stewardship:** Carefully steward financial, material, human, and story-based resources.
- **Accountability:** Hold board, staff, volunteers, and guests accountable to commitments.
- **Responsiveness:** Address urgent community needs with creativity, adaptability, and persistence.

## Organizational Goals
- Help families move quickly into safe, stable housing.
- Expand access and operational efficiency across shelters and programs.
- Reduce manual processes, including bed-availability tracking.
- Improve real-time visibility into resources.
- Maximize social impact and prototype scalable solutions that can extend beyond a single agency.
