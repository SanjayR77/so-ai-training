# Mary's Place — Summary

**One-sentence description:**
Mary's Place is a nonprofit that operates family shelters and housing-stability programs in Seattle, King County to ensure that no child sleeps outside. ([Mary's Place][1])

## Vision

Mary's Place envisions **a community where all families have safety, stability, and housing**, a north star that guides its shelter, prevention, and rapid re-housing efforts across King County. ([Mary's Place][2]) 

## Mission

The organization's mission is to **ensure that no child sleeps outside by centering equity and opportunity for women and families**, delivered through a continuum of services that includes emergency shelter, mobile outreach, diversion, and housing supports that help families regain stable housing. ([Mary's Place][2])

## Core Values

* **Love** — Create spaces where every guest is welcomed, respected, accepted, and cared for.
* **Collaboration** — Partner across the region to expand quality services and elevate family voices.
* **Equity** — Commit to inclusive environments and equitable access and opportunity for all.
* **Stewardship** — Carefully steward financial, material, human, and story-based resources.
* **Accountability** — Keep board, staff, volunteers, and guests accountable to commitments.
* **Responsiveness** — Address urgent community needs with creativity, adaptability, and persistence.

## Goals

Aligned to its mission, Mary's Place focuses on helping families move quickly into **safe, stable housing** while expanding access and operational efficiency across shelters and programs; in the near term, an internal enablement initiative emphasizes reducing manual processes (e.g., bed-availability tracking), improving real-time resource visibility, maximizing social impact, and prototyping **scalable** solutions that can extend beyond a single agency. ([Mary's Place][3])
