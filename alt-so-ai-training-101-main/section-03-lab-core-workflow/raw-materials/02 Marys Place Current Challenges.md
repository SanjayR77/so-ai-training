# Current Challenges Facing Mary's Place

## **Summary of the Opportunities**
- Opportunity to create a streamlined intake system that will help families prioritize and navigate their path to housing security.
- Opportunity to create an inventory management system that tracks real-time family needs across all service areas, enables targeted corporate donations, and facilitates efficient redistribution of inventory to those in need.

## **Shelter Services**
- **Length of stay increasing** due to housing affordability and economic pressures in King County
- **Manual intake process** requiring multiple team assessments (health, housing, youth services) with different staff members
- **Lack of centralized journey mapping** - no way to estimate timeline or sequence complex steps for families
- **Clunky database system** that looks outdated (1990s-era SQL) and requires significant customization
- **Double data entry** - information must be manually entered into both internal system and county-wide HMIS (Homelessness Management Information System)
- **No integration** between internal database and county system

## **Mobile Outreach Services**
- **Limited goods distribution capability** - currently unable to effectively supply household items to outreach families
- **Ad hoc ordering process** - outreach specialists must call warehouse manager to request items with no visibility into inventory
- **Hit-or-miss fulfillment** - families receive whatever is available at the moment rather than what they actually need
- **Short lead times** - often only a few days' notice when families are moving into housing, making it difficult to source needed items

## **Prevention Services**
- **No established system** for distributing goods to prevention families
- **Lack of marketplace access** - prevention families cannot access items like shelter guests can

## **Goods Management & Distribution (Cross-Cutting)**
- **Only 15-20% of donated items are usable** by Mary's Place families
- **No inventory management system** - tracking done manually using bin counts rather than SKU-level detail
- **Unable to target corporate donations** effectively due to lack of real-time needs data
- **Excess unusable inventory** accumulates with no efficient redistribution system to other community organizations
- **Limited storage capacity** at outreach specialist offices
- **Inefficient information flow** between supply (warehouse) and demand (families across all service areas)
- **No centralized demand tracking** across shelter, outreach, and prevention populations
