# Step 1 Worksheet: Signal Assessment

Before working with the agent, review each raw material file and assess its value as context for a TPM planning a software initiative for Mary's Place.

**Signal Rating definitions:**

- **high-signal** — Directly useful for understanding what to build, why, or for whom. Should be processed into structured context.
- **maybe** — Partially useful; may have relevant details buried in noise. Could be summarized or mined selectively.
- **noise** — Redundant, out of scope, or too raw to use meaningfully without significant processing.

---

## Assessment

### `raw-materials/01 Marys Place Organization.md`
- **Signal Rating:** high-signal
- **Rationale:** Establishes the organization's mission, values, and focus on helping families reach stable housing. This explains why the work matters and who it serves, though it does not define detailed product requirements.
- **Action:** Keep the mission, values, and service-area context as background. Verify initiative-specific claims against the challenges and stakeholder materials before treating them as requirements.

### `raw-materials/02 Marys Place Current Challenges.md`
- **Signal Rating:** high-signal
- **Rationale:** Describes concrete problems across shelter intake, outreach, prevention, and goods distribution, including manual work, duplicate entry, and gaps in inventory visibility. It directly informs what might be improved and for whom.
- **Action:** Organize the challenges by service area and user group, then turn them into problem statements. Validate workflows, impact, and priorities with the people who do the work and the families affected.

### `raw-materials/03 Intro Call with Marys Place.txt`
- **Signal Rating:** high-signal
- **Rationale:** Stakeholders explain current processes, desired outcomes, and two potential opportunities: a family-centered shelter journey-planning tool and improved goods distribution. The transcript adds useful nuance, but is long, noisy, and includes ideas that have not yet been validated.
- **Action:** Summarize by stakeholder, current workflow, pain point, desired outcome, and open question. Separate confirmed needs from proposed solutions, and preserve important constraints such as staff collaboration, multiple languages, and existing data systems.

## Reflection Questions

After completing the table, consider the following before moving to Step 2:

1. The intro-call transcript will take the most effort because it is long, has transcription noise, and moves between two distinct opportunities. It will take careful summarizing to separate confirmed needs from early ideas.
2. I would want to learn more about privacy, consent, data access and retention, and how any tool would safely handle sensitive family information. I would also ask how success will be measured, who would own and maintain the solution, and which workflows and system integrations are priorities.
3. For the proposed shelter journey-planning tool, families staying in shelter are the primary users: it should help them understand and prioritize steps toward stable housing. Their housing specialist and health and youth-services staff would also use or contribute to the plan. I have moderate confidence because this is an early concept from one call, and the materials also describe a separate goods-distribution opportunity with different users.

> **Note:** There are no wrong answers here. The goal is to build the habit of *evaluating* your sources before asking an AI to synthesize them.
