## Target Users (Personas)

### 1. Family in Emergency Shelter
- **Context:** A family working with health, housing, and youth-services teams to move toward stable housing.
- **Needs:** Clear, prioritized next steps; a flexible sense of progress and timeline; communication in a familiar language and style.
- **Pain points:** Multiple assessments, complex steps, and uncertainty about what to do first or how long the journey may take.
- **Goal:** Return to stable housing with a manageable plan.

### 2. Family Served Outside Shelter
- **Context:** A family receiving mobile outreach while living in a car or tent, or prevention support while at imminent risk of losing housing.
- **Needs:** A way to navigate relevant services and, if validated, request or access household goods.
- **Pain points:** Outreach and prevention families have less access to goods and coordinated support than shelter guests.
- **Goal:** Stabilize or move into housing with the support and goods they need.

### 3. Housing Specialist / Central Navigator
- **Context:** A staff member who coordinates a family's overall case and connects them with health and youth-services teams.
- **Needs:** A shared view of family goals, barriers, and next steps; better sequencing of time-sensitive actions.
- **Pain points:** Separate assessments and a clunky shared data system make coordination harder.
- **Goal:** Help families make progress while coordinating staff support effectively.

### 4. Health and Youth-Services Staff
- **Context:** Team members who assess health coverage and care needs, or help families navigate schooling and transportation.
- **Needs:** Relevant family information and visibility into coordinated goals and progress.
- **Pain points:** Their assessments are part of a complex process involving different staff and teams.
- **Goal:** Address their area of a family's needs at the appropriate point in the housing journey.

### 5. Warehouse / In-Kind Donations Staff
- **Context:** Staff who receive, sort, value, count, and distribute donated goods.
- **Needs:** Better visibility into inventory and family demand across service areas.
- **Pain points:** Inventory is tracked by category and bin rather than by item; requests can be ad hoc, and unusable surplus accumulates.
- **Goal:** Match available goods to actual family needs and help route surplus usefully.
